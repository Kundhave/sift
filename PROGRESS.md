## How to resume / launch
- Launch: `make demo` from the repo root (builds the UI, starts CPU-only backend :8000 and frontend :5173, prints the URL; logs in `.run/`). Stop with `make stop`.
- Tests: `make e2e` (Playwright, CPU); pytest suite via `PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python -m pytest` (about 12 min on CPU).
- Tables/graphs: `make reproduce` (baselines + make_tables.py). Latency: `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES='' PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python experiments/latency_full.py --device cpu`, then `--device cuda`. GPU retraining: experiments/run_all_gpu.sh (hours).
